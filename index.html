Lafayette Days

Mt. Diablo Boulevard, the reservoir loop, and hills that go gold in summer, green in winter, and flower in spring.

  • Pick Milo or Juniper. They remember you next time.
  • Walk, take off on foot, ride a bike, drive, or take the helicopter.
  • WASD to move, F or a double-tap of Space to fly, Esc to swap kids.
  • Landmarks chime when you find them, and the Hall of Builders fills in with the people behind each place.
  • A glowing door at a landmark steps into a scanned memory of the place.
  • Esc pauses. The town clock there can wind the year back to 1955.
  • Gems and Explorer notes are optional. The town is still the point.
